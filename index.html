Daily Confessions

Short storytelling videos about confessions, relationship drama and everyday conflicts.

Daily Confessions publishes narrated short stories set over gameplay footage. Each story is either an original fictional story written for the channel, or a story a viewer sent us and allowed us to share, with names and details changed. Send us a story

Have a confession or story you would like featured anonymously? Email confessdailythreads@gmail.com. We only use stories with the sender's permission and change names and identifying details.

How our YouTube publishing tool works

We use our own internal tool, connected to our YouTube channel through Google's YouTube API Services, to upload and schedule the videos we make and to read our own channel's performance statistics. Only the Daily Confessions team uses it, for the Daily Confessions channel; it is not offered to anyone else, and no other person signs in with Google. Every video is reviewed and approved by us before it is published.

Read exactly what Google data the tool accesses, how it is stored and how to delete it in our Privacy Policy.
