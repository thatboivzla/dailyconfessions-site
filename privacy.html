Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

This policy explains what information Daily Confessions ("we", "us") handles through this website, through email, and through our internal publishing tool that connects to our own YouTube channel. Questions: confessdailythreads@gmail.com.

1. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or forms, and it does not load anything from third-party servers. Like any website, our hosting provider may record standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) in its server logs for security and operation; we do not use those logs to identify visitors.

2. Emails and story submissions

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use it only to reply and, if you send a story and give permission, to consider it for a video with names and identifying details changed. We do not sell or share submissions. Ask us at any time to delete your email and submission.

3. Google user data (YouTube API Services)

Our publishing tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the account holder agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who this applies to

The tool is used only by the Daily Confessions team and connects only to the Google account that owns the Daily Confessions YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and no other Google users sign in to it. We never receive data about the people who watch our videos beyond the totals that YouTube reports for our own channel.

What we access, and why

Permission (scope)What we accessWhy
youtube.upload Upload videos we created to our channel, set their privacy and scheduled publish time, change or cancel a scheduled time, and mark them as containing AI-generated (synthetic) content. To publish the videos we have reviewed and approved, at the times we choose.
youtube.readonly Our channel's ID, name and subscriber count; the upload, processing and privacy status of videos our tool uploaded. To confirm the right channel is connected and that each upload succeeded.
yt-analytics.readonly Totals for our own videos: views, engaged views, average view duration, average percentage viewed, likes, comment count, shares and subscribers gained. To measure how our videos perform and improve future videos.

We do not access viewers' identities or personal information, comment text, messages, other channels, or your Google account's email, contacts or other Google services.

How we use it

  • Only to run our own channel as described above: publishing approved videos, checking upload status and reviewing our own performance statistics.
  • We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it except with the service providers listed below that run our tool for us.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • Only the Daily Confessions team can view the data.

Daily Confessions' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How it is stored and protected

  • Sign-in credentials: the long-lived YouTube sign-in (refresh token) and our channel ID and name are stored encrypted in our private cloud work environment's settings. The decryption key is stored separately and is set only by the account owner. The short-lived access token is kept only in a temporary, access-restricted file in that private environment.
  • App credentials: our Google OAuth client secret is kept in the cloud platform's credential store and is never written into our code.
  • Statistics: the video statistics listed above are stored in our private project database, kept in a private code repository, and shown on a private dashboard that only the account owner can open.
  • Credentials are never published, committed to code, or written to logs. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Service providers

  • Google: provides the YouTube APIs our tool calls.
  • Anthropic (Claude Code cloud environment): runs our tool and processes this data on our behalf.
  • GitHub: stores our private project repository, including the statistics database.

Retention and deletion

  • We keep the sign-in only while our channel is connected, and statistics only while we operate the channel.
  • When access is revoked, or when you ask us, we delete the stored sign-in right away and delete the stored YouTube statistics from our active database within 7 days, including older copies kept in our private project history within 30 days.
  • Revoke access any time in your Google Account's security settings: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions (Third-party apps & services). Revoking stops all access immediately.
  • To request deletion, email confessdailythreads@gmail.com.

4. TikTok and Instagram

We publish to our own TikTok and Instagram accounts through Zernio, a social-media scheduling service, under those platforms' rules. This involves our own accounts' usernames, post status and post statistics only, and does not involve any Google user data.

5. Children

Our website and tool are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

6. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
